test(desks): keep the fixture alive past the run; claim usage only when observed

Root cause of the Unix CI failure was fixture lifetime, not slow startup:
the scoped lane closure moved the Tmp and dropped it on return, deleting
helper.pid before the parent asserted. The parent now owns the fixture and
the thread gets a cloned root; the pid captured before the deadline is
reused for the final liveness check. The 1 s desk deadline and tight marker
window are restored, with a hard total bound of 3 s (deadline + 2 s) so a
reintroduced pipe-grace regression cannot hide. Helper marker and .error
surfacing and the product tree-kill path are unchanged.

F11: usage the answer reports is no longer recorded as observed usage. Only
a usage block seen in the CLI events becomes the usage field; an
answer-supplied one is kept under claimed, with a test for that case.
